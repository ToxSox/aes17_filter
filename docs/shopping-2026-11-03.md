- **No enclosures for now.** The boards stand on their spacers; the Hammond 1444-862 and 1434-86

All lines were in stock, 1-2 working days.

**Two deviations from hardware/README.md:**

- The 220 pF is the ±2.5 % part, 40 pieces, which the README allows if the ±1 % is not to be had.
  The ±1 % is stocked only at Bürklin (42D8516, 33 pieces 58.74 € net). Digi-Key's ±2.5 % has a
  24 000-piece minimum.
- The 4.7 nF is the 630 V ±2.5 % FKP2J014701I00HSSD instead of the 250 V ±5 % FKP2F014701I00JA00.
  Same body (7.2 × 6.5 × 8.0 mm, 5 mm pitch), half the tolerance, so pairing is easier. The
  250 V ±5 % equivalent is FKP2-250 4,7N at 0.24 €.

The toggle wire and the amplifier cable are from stock; the toggle wires can see 75 Vrms (106 V peak), so the wire must
be rated above that. The wire bridges on the three boards without a toggle are cut resistor leads.

## Not priced: from a hardware store

- 70 M3 × 6 screws, steel, 35 now and 35 for the bottom plates of a later box. Reichelt's steel M3
  screws start at 10 mm, and in a box two of those collide in the 10 mm spacer: M3 × 6 from both
  sides leaves 0.9 mm between them. Its nylon SP M3X6 (8.25 €, 100 pieces) would do except at H5,
  which needs metal for the ground contact.
- two small aluminium brackets for the toggles, with a 6.5 mm hole
